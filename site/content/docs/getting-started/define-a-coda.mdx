A [Coda](/docs/method/primitives#coda) describes what will be true when the work succeeds and why that matters.

## Draft the outcome

1. **Name who benefits.** Look at your Notes and identify who is affected by the current problem.
2. **Write what success looks like.** Describe what those people can reliably do or experience once the change is in place.
3. **Decide what would show it is true.** Name something you can observe, such as whether an account lead finds the current status in the estimate record.
4. **Connect it to the outcome above it.** If the System sits under a Portfolio, check that its Coda serves that Portfolio Coda. A standalone System has a root Coda.

## Worked example

```text title="Example Coda"
Account leads can tell whether a client estimate is ready to send by looking at its record. They can act on approved estimates without chasing a status update.
```

Ask someone who knows the work what they would look for to verify that statement. Capture unresolved assumptions or disagreements in Notes.

## Save your output [#save-output]

### Work with documents — recommended [#save-documents]

Save the agreed System Coda in your initiative document or `system-coda.md`, with the relevant Notes and open questions. Give it a clear name or link so you can refer to it from each capability draft. If it changes later, preserve the previous statement and record why it changed.

<details id="save-repository">
<summary>Save in a repository</summary>

Ask your coding agent to create or update `harmonic-method/coda.yml` using the kit's Coda and System specs. Give the Coda a stable identifier for later references. For a standalone System, omit its parent; if it serves an existing Portfolio Coda, reference that known Coda.

Now review the initial capture in `working-notes.md` and attach confirmed System Notes using valid identifiers and the Note spec. Keep unresolved material in the working document. Validate the resulting tree using the [repository workflow](/docs/implementation/use-a-repository#validate).

</details>
