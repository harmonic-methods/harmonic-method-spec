Run a quality check once you have a draft Coda. In this walkthrough, you'll check the estimate approval status Beat and see what would make its promise clearer.

## Run the review

1. **Choose the rubric.** Use Coda Quality for a Coda with a parent, including the example Beat. Use Root Coda Quality for a Coda with no parent. See the [quality model](/docs/method/quality#gates) for the available checks.
2. **Gather the evidence.** For the Beat, bring its title, Coda, and description, plus the parent Coda, sibling Beats, and relevant Notes you have. For a root Coda, bring the statement and the Notes and other records that support its claims.
3. **Apply the rubric as written.** Work through it by hand or provide the rubric and those materials in your organization's approved AI workspace. If you have the starter kit, the [sample review instructions](/docs/getting-started#starter-kit) show how to supply files. If you do not have the full rubric, use the example below to practise reviewing the wording before scoring.
4. **Read the reasons and decide what to change.** Keep the rubric result with the spec set version and inputs used. Have the responsible person review the suggested improvements. Recheck the Coda after a material change.

## Worked example

Bring the Beat and its parent Coda from the earlier steps, along with the Note about the designated approver. For **Clarity**, look at the phrase “current approval state.” A reader may still wonder how to tell whether an estimate has been approved. One revision is to say that account leads can tell from the estimate record whether approval is pending, granted, or declined. Ask about any sibling Beats you haven't seen before drawing a conclusion about overlap.

When you're ready to deliver the capability, [plan Beat Versions and deliver Revisions](/docs/implementation/plan-beat-versions).
