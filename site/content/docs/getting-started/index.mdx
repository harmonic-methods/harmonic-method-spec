## Start with the basics [#starter-kit]

<StarterKitDownload />

Once you have the ZIP, unzip it and open [`START_HERE.md`](https://github.com/harmonic-methods/harmonic-method-spec/blob/main/starter-kit/START_HERE.md).

### 1. Try the sample review

Attach [`review-a-capability.md`](/downloads/starter-kit/review-a-capability.md) and [`sample-capability.md`](/downloads/starter-kit/sample-capability.md) to a new chat, then paste this prompt. You get a score and reason for each dimension, an overall verdict, and one practical improvement.

```text title="Prompt: Review a document"
Follow the Harmonic Method rubric in review-a-capability.md to assess the proposed capability in the other attachment. Cite the document in your reasons and recommend the single most useful improvement. Flag missing context instead of making it up.
```

<details>
<summary>Using an AI assistant that can read a folder?</summary>

Open the extracted kit in your approved AI app and grant it access to that folder. You can run the same review with this prompt:

```text title="Prompt: Review a local file"
Read skills/coda-quality-check/SKILL.md and follow it to review the capability in sample-capability.md. Read the referenced specs, report the rubric's results, and recommend the single most useful improvement. Leave the source files as they are.
```

</details>

### 2. Choose one initiative

Pick a real piece of work and bring a brief, meeting recap, or short description of the problem. Identify who should benefit and what you want to improve. You can also continue with the client estimate example used in the guides.

## Apply the method to your initiative [#walkthrough]

1. [Capture Notes](/docs/getting-started/capture-notes): save the facts, constraints, guidance, and decisions you know.
2. [Define a Coda](/docs/getting-started/define-a-coda): save the broader outcome the initiative serves.
3. [Define a Capability Beat](/docs/getting-started/define-a-beat): save one durable capability, its outcome, and its connection to the System Coda.
4. [Run a Coda check](/docs/getting-started/run-a-coda-check): save the assessment and decide what to improve.

Each guide tells you what to bring, what to do, and how to save its output in either path.

## Keep the method in your assistant's instructions [#assistant-instructions]

For recurring work, you can save a short instruction in your assistant workspace or a repository's `AGENTS.md`. [Use the copyable setup instructions](/docs/appendix/llm-setup#method-instructions) and provide the relevant method files and initiative context.
