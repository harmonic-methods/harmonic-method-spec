## Try the open spec kit [#starter-kit]

<StarterKitDownload />

Once you have the ZIP, unzip it and open `START_HERE.md`. The worked example takes you through a proposed capability and returns a score for each dimension, an overall verdict, and one practical improvement.

### Review one document in chat

1. Attach `review-a-capability.md` and `sample-capability.md` to a new chat.
2. Paste the prompt below. To review your own work, attach your document in place of the sample. Include the broader outcome it serves if you have it.

> Follow the Harmonic Method rubric in review-a-capability.md to assess the proposed capability in the other attachment. Cite the document in your reasons and recommend the single most useful improvement. Flag missing context instead of making it up.

### Work with a local folder

If your approved desktop AI app can read local folders, you can run the same review from the extracted kit.

1. Open the extracted folder in your approved desktop AI app and grant it access to the folder.
2. Paste the prompt below. To review your own work, put copies in `your-files/` and name the document you want reviewed.

> Read skills/coda-quality-check/SKILL.md and follow it to review the capability in sample-capability.md. Read the referenced specs, report the rubric's results, and recommend the single most useful improvement. Leave the source files as they are.

Decisions and approvals stay with the people responsible for the work.

## Apply the method to a new initiative [#walkthrough]

Follow the same client estimate example across all four guides, or apply each step to your own initiative.

1. [Capture Notes](/docs/getting-started/capture-notes) about what you know, the boundaries you face, and the decisions you've made.
2. [Define a Coda](/docs/getting-started/define-a-coda) that describes what success looks like.
3. [Define a Beat](/docs/getting-started/define-a-beat) that makes part of that outcome possible.
4. [Run a Coda check](/docs/getting-started/run-a-coda-check) to decide what to improve.
