---
title: The Harmonic Method
description: Keep the work connected to the outcome it is meant to achieve.
---

The Harmonic Method helps teams agree on what should change, decide what they need to make it happen, and keep track of why they made those choices.

[Getting started](/docs/getting-started) walks you through a sample review and shows how to apply the method to your own work.

[Explore the method](/docs/method/overview) for the concepts and roles behind the steps.
