---
title: The Harmonic Method
description: Clearer goals, less wasted effort, and continuity across handoffs.
---

The Harmonic Method helps people and AI agents keep work connected to its purpose. It makes outcomes explicit, preserves decision rationale, and carries context across teams and sessions.

[Getting started](/docs/getting-started) walks you through a sample review and applying the method to your own work.

## The problem it addresses [#problem]

Intent gets lost between strategy, planning, and execution. People repeat explanations, agents rebuild context, and completed tasks miss the goal. The result is **decision debt**: the gap between what an organization meant to build and what it can account for having built.

## Common challenges and intended benefits [#challenges-and-benefits]

These practices aim to reduce wasted human and AI effort. Benefits are intended, not measured or guaranteed.

| Common challenge | Method practice | Intended benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Different interpretations of success | Explicit outcomes, constraints, and testable steps | Clearer goals and less rework |
| Context lost across handoffs | Durable Notes that preserve context and rationale | Continuity across people, agents, and sessions |
| Repeated explanations and rediscovery | Reusable context attached to the work | Less human time and AI effort spent rebuilding understanding |
| Unnoticed changes in direction | Regular alignment reviews and recorded reference points | Earlier drift detection and fewer surprises |
| Repeated debate or unclear ownership | Recorded decisions and six accountable roles | Faster decisions and fewer coordination gaps |
| Activity disconnected from value | Work traced to the outcome it serves | Clearer priorities and reasons to continue, change, or stop |

**More efficient token usage is an intended benefit.** Reusable context aims to reduce speculative attempts and correction loops. Preparing and reviewing it also costs tokens; savings need measurement per accepted outcome.

## Structure of the method [#structure]

A **Coda** states the outcome. **Capability Beats**, or **Beats**, describe durable capabilities. **Beat Versions** define testable steps in a Beat’s evolution, and **Revisions** deliver the changes. **Notes** preserve what informs the work. [Explore the primitives →](/docs/method/primitives)

Six roles hold the work together at any scope, from a capability to a portfolio:

- [Director](/docs/method/roles#director): sets direction and tests whether it still holds.
- [Composer](/docs/method/roles#composer): defines coherent systems and capabilities serving that direction.
- [Orchestrator](/docs/method/roles#orchestrator): sequences work and assigns people and agents.
- [Conductor](/docs/method/roles#conductor): leads day-to-day execution within agreed boundaries.
- [Producer](/docs/method/roles#producer): decides whether the work is worth its cost.
- [Promoter](/docs/method/roles#promoter): helps the intended audience understand and adopt it.

## Applying the method [#adoption]

These are functions, not job titles. One person may hold several; the useful question is which function is unowned. Start with one capability and [try the method](/docs/getting-started).
