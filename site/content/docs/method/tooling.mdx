You can practise the method by hand or use an agent to draft material, check it against the published rubrics, and surface decisions for the people accountable for the work.

## What agents can help with [#assistants]

The published skills help you draft a Capability Beat, review a Beat Version plan, reconcile a Coda chain, or take a Downbeat. Some functions share tools. Each role still needs someone who can make its decisions.

[See the six roles](/docs/method/roles) or [choose a quality check](/docs/method/quality#gates).

## Recommended skills by role [#role-skills]

Agents can assist every role. We recommend giving them explicit skills for their assigned work. A skill should state the context to read, outputs to produce, checks to apply, actions permitted, and decisions to escalate. The accountable person sets the agent's authority; selecting a role or loading a skill grants no additional permission.

| Role | Published skills in the spec kit | Additional skills a team could define |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Director | No dedicated Director skill yet | Review strategic assumptions, compare options, and monitor business signals for human review |
| Composer | `beat-composing`, `beat-clarifying`, `coda-quality-check`, `coverage-reading` | Apply domain-specific architecture and capability boundaries |
| Orchestrator | `beat-version-composing`, `beat-version-quality-check` | Plan capacity, dependencies, workflow policies, and escalation paths |
| Conductor | `coda-alignment-check`, `downbeat-taking` | Triage current work, surface blockers, and coordinate execution within delegated limits |
| Producer | `root-coda-composing`, `root-coda-quality-check` | Compare investment alternatives and prepare value-and-cost reviews |
| Promoter | No dedicated Promoter skill yet | Draft audience-specific communication, enable users, and review adoption evidence |

Published skills assess or prepare material for the named role; they do not cover every responsibility it holds. For example, Root Coda Quality helps Producer assess value claims, while Director remains accountable for the validity of strategic direction. An agent can assist several roles, with the relevant context and authority made explicit for each task.

The additional skills above are recommendations, not published instruments or required certifications. Start with the [AI assistant setup guide](/docs/appendix/llm-setup) to use a published skill or save a team-specific assistant.
