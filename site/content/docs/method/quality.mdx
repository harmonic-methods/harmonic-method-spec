Use the published rubrics to make the quality of a capability or plan visible before work moves forward.

## Explore, compose, and execute [#phases]

1. **Explore.** Establish the destination and capture the Notes that explain the reasoning and constraints.
2. **Compose.** Define the capabilities that serve the Coda. Assess each Beat and check that the set covers the outcome with minimal overlap.
3. **Execute.** Plan bounded Beat Versions and deliver them through Revisions. Keep recording decisions as the work evolves.

## The seven Coda quality dimensions [#dimensions]

The Coda quality check assesses an entity whose Coda has a parent. It scores each dimension from 1 to 5.

  <div className="cajon-quality-item"><strong>Distinctive</strong><p>The outcome stands apart from its siblings' promises.</p></div>
  <div className="cajon-quality-item"><strong>Harmonious</strong><p>The capability works with its siblings and respects shared constraints.</p></div>
  <div className="cajon-quality-item"><strong>Substantial</strong><p>It represents a meaningful whole that earns its own boundary.</p></div>
  <div className="cajon-quality-item"><strong>Durable</strong><p>It can evolve as the work beneath it advances the outcome.</p></div>
  <div className="cajon-quality-item"><strong>Clear</strong><p>A reader can understand what it delivers and why it matters.</p></div>
  <div className="cajon-quality-item"><strong>Strategically Aligned</strong><p>It serves the outcome its immediate parent Coda describes.</p></div>

Read the set as well as its parts. A coverage review can show where related capabilities duplicate each other or where part of the system Coda has no capability serving it.

## Which check to use [#gates]

| Check | What it examines |
| --- | --- |
| Coda Quality | A Coda with a parent, together with the entity carrying it |
| Root Coda Quality | A Coda with no parent and the evidence for its claims |
| Beat Version Plan Quality | The plan's scope, direction, testability, traceability, assignability, and risks |
| Coda Alignment | Findings about whether a chain of Codas still fits together |

The reference implementation uses a **4.0** passing threshold for the Coda and plan assessments. Teams choose how they act on results. An implementation can apply a gate to its own workflow.

## Keep the evidence with the result [#why-gates]

Record the spec set and the inputs the assessment used. When a plan, Coda, or material context changes, review it again before relying on a previous result. The people accountable for the work decide how to respond.
