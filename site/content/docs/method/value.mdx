<p className="cajon-page-lead">The Producer's function. It covers selection and funding — what gets made, and whether it earns its keep. Whether the work then reaches its audience belongs to the <a href="/docs/method/roles#promoter">Promoter</a>.</p>

## The question [#question]

<p>Does this earn its keep? What gets funded, and what gets made?</p>

<p>The other functions assume the work is worth doing and ask how to do it well. The Producer is the function that can decline. Without it, a portfolio accumulates capabilities that are individually well-built and collectively unjustified.</p>

## At each altitude [#altitudes]

<p>Altitude is a property of the work, not of the role, so the Producer's question is the same at every level and only the unit changes.</p>

<div className="hm-docs-table" tabIndex={0} role="region" aria-label="Comparison table">
<table className="hm-table">
        <thead><tr><th>Altitude</th><th>The Producer's question</th></tr></thead>
        <tbody>
          <tr><td>System</td><td>Does this system earn its keep — does the capability it provides justify its continued cost and attention?</td></tr>

<p>The portfolio question is the one no individual team can answer from inside their own system. Without a shared map, teams optimize locally while the portfolio drifts.</p>

## What portfolio altitude makes visible [#portfolio]

<ul>
        <li>Which capabilities exist across the portfolio, and where they overlap or conflict.</li>
        <li>How current initiatives relate to strategic priorities — and how much of the portfolio is misaligned.</li>
        <li>When a new initiative is proposed: where it fits, what it depends on, and what it affects.</li>
        <li>How resources get allocated when every team has legitimate competing claims.</li>
      </ul>

## Selection is a standing function, not a planning event [#standing]

<p>Funding decisions made once a year and revisited only under pressure are not selection — they are budgeting. The Producer function is continuous for the same reason the Conductor's is: the conditions that justified a capability change while the work is underway, and the decision to keep going is a decision whether or not anyone makes it deliberately.</p>
