## What Harmonic Portfolio Management addresses

Harmonic Composition gives a single system coherence. Harmonic Portfolio Management gives a collection of systems strategic coherence. It answers the questions that no individual team can see from inside their own system:

- Which capabilities exist across the portfolio, and where do they overlap or conflict?
- How do the current initiatives relate to strategic priorities — and how much of the portfolio is misaligned?
- When a new initiative is proposed, where does it fit, what does it depend on, and what does it affect?
- How do resources get allocated across systems when every team has legitimate competing claims?
